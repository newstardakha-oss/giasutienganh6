## Quy trình viết chung (áp dụng cho mọi Unit)

Để viết một bài hoàn chỉnh, các em hãy làm theo 4 bước đơn giản sau:

- **Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)**: Trước khi viết, hãy tự đặt và trả lời các câu hỏi "Wh" để tìm ý tưởng. Viết các ý tưởng bằng từ khóa hoặc câu ngắn để dễ sắp xếp. Bên dưới chỉ là một số ví dụ, các em hãy suy nghĩ để có thêm nhiều ý tưởng khác.
- **Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)**: Một bài viết thường có 3 phần. Hãy sắp xếp các ý tưởng của các em theo cấu trúc sau:
- **Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)**: Dựa vào dàn ý, hãy viết một bài hoàn chỉnh vào giấy nháp. Ở bước này, đừng quá lo lắng về lỗi sai, hãy tập trung diễn đạt ý tưởng của mình.
- **Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)**: Đọc lại bài viết của các em và kiểm tra các lỗi sau:
  - Spelling: Lỗi chính tả.
  - Grammar: Lỗi ngữ pháp.
  - Punctuation: Dấu câu (dấu chấm, dấu phẩy).
  - Clarity: Nội dung đã rõ ràng và dễ hiểu chưa?

## Unit 1 – My New School

**Topic:** Write a paragraph of 40-50 words about your school.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **What?** – What is the name of your school? (*Trường của em tên là gì?*)
- **Where?** – Where is your school located? (*Trường của em nằm ở đâu?*)
- **How many?** – How many classes does your school have? (*Trường của em có bao nhiêu lớp học?*)
- **What?** – What do students do at your school? (*Học sinh làm gì ở trường?*)
- **Why?** – Why do you like your school? (*Tại sao em lại thích trường của mình?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Topic Sentence)**: Giới thiệu tên và vị trí trường của em.  
   Ví dụ: *My school is Hoa Binh Lower Secondary School and it is in a small town.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Miêu tả số lớp học, cơ sở vật chất và các hoạt động của học sinh ở trường.  
   Ví dụ: *It has 20 classes. We study interesting subjects and play sports in the afternoon.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Concluding Sentence)**: Nêu cảm nghĩ của em về trường học.  
   Ví dụ: *I really love my school because the teachers are very friendly.*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **School types:** boarding school, international school, lower secondary school
- **Places at school:** computer room, library, school garden, playground, classroom, schoolyard
- **Activities:** study subjects, do homework, join clubs, play sports, talk with friends, do exercise
- **Adjectives:** large, beautiful, quiet, modern, comfortable, friendly, kind
- **Sentence starters:**
  - My school is...
  - It is located in...
  - My school has...
  - At my school, students usually...
  - I really love my school because...

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (106 từ)

My school is Chu Van An Lower Secondary School, which is located in a quiet area in Ha Noi. It is a large and beautiful school with thirty classes and many green trees.

My school has a big library, modern computer rooms, and a large playground where we often gather. During the day, we study interesting subjects like maths, science, and English. In the afternoon, students often join different clubs such as the sports club or the art club. I usually play football with my friends after lessons.

I really love my school because it has great teachers and it is a wonderful place to learn.

*Sample Paragraph 2* (103 từ)

I study at Binh Thuy Lower Secondary School, a nice and friendly school in Can Tho. Our school is not very big, but it has twenty comfortable classes and a beautiful school garden.

At school, students study many subjects and do exercises every day. We also have a small library to read books. At break time, we often sit on the benches to talk or play fun games in the schoolyard. The teachers here are very kind and they always help us with our lessons.

I am very proud of my school and I enjoy every day I spend here with my friends.

### II. PRACTICE

Write a complete paragraph: *Write a paragraph of 40-50 words about your school.*

## Unit 2 – My House

**Topic:** Write an email to Mira, your pen friend, to tell her about your house.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **Where?** – Where do you live? (*Em sống ở đâu?*)
- **What?** – What type of house is it? (*Đó là kiểu nhà gì?*)
- **How many?** – How many rooms are there in your house? (*Có bao nhiêu phòng trong nhà em?*)
- **Which?** – Which room do you like the best? (*Em thích căn phòng nào nhất?*)
- **Why?** – Why do you like it? (*Tại sao em thích căn phòng đó?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Greeting)**: Gửi lời chào và giới thiệu về ngôi nhà của em.  
   Ví dụ: *Hi Mira, thanks for your email. Now I'll tell you about my house.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Miêu tả các phòng trong nhà và căn phòng em yêu thích nhất.  
   Ví dụ: *I live in a town house with four rooms. My favourite room is my bedroom because it is very cozy.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Closing)**: Hỏi thăm về ngôi nhà của bạn và gửi lời chào tạm biệt.  
   Ví dụ: *What about you? Where do you live? Tell me in your next email.*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **Types of house:** town house, country house, flat, villa
- **Rooms:** living room, bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, hall
- **Furniture:** sofa, bed, wardrobe, chest of drawers, cupboard, fridge, lamp
- **Adjectives:** big, small, beautiful, cozy, clean, comfortable, bright
- **Sentence starters:**
  - I live in a...
  - There are... rooms in my house.
  - My favourite room is...
  - I like it because...
  - What about you? Where...

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (112 từ)

Hi Mira, thanks for your email.

Now I'll tell you about my house. I live in a beautiful town house in Ha Noi with my parents and my younger brother.

There are six rooms in my house: a living room, a kitchen, three bedrooms, and a bathroom. My favourite room is my bedroom because it is very cozy. There is a comfortable bed next to the window, a bookshelf on the wall, and a desk where I do my homework. I love my room because it is my private space to relax and read books.

What about you? Where do you live? Tell me in your next email.

All the best, Linh.

*Sample Paragraph 2* (103 từ)

Hi Mira,

Thanks for your email. I am very happy to tell you about my house. My family lives in a modern flat in Ho Chi Minh City.

My flat has a spacious living room, a kitchen, two bedrooms, and two bathrooms. I like the living room the best in my house. It is very bright because there is a big window. There is a big sofa, a television, and a beautiful lamp. We often sit there together to watch TV and chat after dinner.

How many rooms are there in your house? Please tell me in your next email.

Best wishes, Khoa.

### II. PRACTICE

Write a complete paragraph: *Write an email to Mira, your pen friend, to tell her about your house.*

## Unit 3 – My Friends

**Topic:** Write a diary entry of about 50-80 words about your best friend.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **Who?** – Who is your best friend? (*Bạn thân nhất của em là ai?*)
- **What?** – What does he / she look like? (*Bạn ấy trông như thế nào?*)
- **What?** – What is he / she like? (*Tính cách của bạn ấy ra sao?*)
- **What?** – What do you usually do together? (*Các em thường làm gì cùng nhau?*)
- **Why?** – Why do you like him / her? (*Tại sao em lại yêu quý bạn ấy?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Topic Sentence)**: Giới thiệu người bạn thân nhất của em.  
   Ví dụ: *My best friend is Tuan, who is my classmate at school.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Miêu tả ngoại hình, tính cách của bạn ấy và những việc các em làm cùng nhau.  
   Ví dụ: *He is tall and has short black hair. He is very kind and always helps me with my homework.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Concluding Sentence)**: Nêu lý do em yêu quý bạn ấy và cảm nghĩ của em.  
   Ví dụ: *I love Tuan because he is a great listener, and I hope we will always be good friends.*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **Appearance:** tall, short, thin, round face, short hair, big eyes
- **Personality:** friendly, kind, clever, creative, active, hard-working
- **Shared Activities:** play football, study together, ride bikes, share snacks, talk about hobbies
- **Feelings & Relationships:** like, love, trust, admire, look after, help each other
- **Time & Frequency:** every day, at the weekend, in our free time, always, usually
- **Sentence starters:**
  - My best friend is...
  - He / She has... and...
  - What I like most about him / her is...
  - We often... together.
  - I hope we will...

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (115 từ)

My best friend is Khanh, who lives in Hanoi, the capital of Vietnam. We have been close friends since we were in primary school.

Khanh is a tall boy with a round face, bright eyes, and short black hair. He is very clever, creative, and always helpful. In our free time, we love reading comic books and playing badminton in the park near West Lake. He is also very good at maths and often helps me solve difficult problems after class.

I like to be friend with Khanh because he is always honest and knows how to make me laugh when I am sad. I feel extremely lucky to have him as my best friend.

*Sample Paragraph 2* (102 từ)

My best friend is Nhi, who goes to school with me in Ho Chi Minh City. We live in the same apartment building in District 3.

Nhi is quite short and thin, with long straight hair and a friendly smile. She is active, sporty, and extremely caring. Every afternoon, we cycle to a nearby sports centre to play table tennis and chat. She always shares her sweet treats and tells me funny jokes.

I really admire Nhi because she is a great listener who always supports me when I face challenges. I hope our friendship will last for a very long time.

## Unit 4 – My Neighbourhood

**Topic:** Write a paragraph of about 50 words about your neighbourhood, saying what you like and dislike about it.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **Where?** – Where is your neighbourhood? (*Khu phố của em nằm ở đâu?*)
- **What?** – What does it look like? (*Khu phố đó trông như thế nào?*)
- **Who?** – What are the people there like? (*Mọi người ở đó như thế nào?*)
- **What?** – What do you like and dislike about your neighbourhood? (*Em thích và không thích những điểm gì ở khu phố của mình?*)
- **How?** – How do you feel about living there? (*Em cảm thấy thế nào khi sống ở đó?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Topic Sentence)**: Giới thiệu khu phố nơi em sống.  
   Ví dụ: *I live in a peaceful neighbourhood in the suburbs of Ha Noi.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Kể về những điều em thích và không thích ở khu phố của mình.  
   Ví dụ: *I like it because there are many green trees and friendly people, but I dislike the lack of modern shopping malls.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Concluding Sentence)**: Nêu cảm nghĩ chung của em về khu phố đó.  
   Ví dụ: *Overall, I still love my neighbourhood because it is a safe and comfortable place to live.*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **Places & Facilities:** park, playground, market, convenience store, cafe, lake, narrow street, modern building
- **Positive Adjectives:** peaceful, quiet, clean, green, friendly, helpful, convenient, safe
- **Negative Adjectives:** noisy, busy, crowded, polluted, dirty, far, heavy traffic
- **Activities:** play outdoor games, go for a walk, ride a bicycle, buy fresh food, chat with neighbours
- **Sentence starters:**
  - I live in...
  - The thing I like most about my neighbourhood is...
  - In my neighbourhood, there are...
  - However, I dislike... because...
  - Overall, I feel... living here.

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (108 từ)

I live in a peaceful valley village in Hoa Lu District, Ninh Binh Province.

There are many things I love about my neighbourhood. It is surrounded by stunning limestone mountains and green rice fields, which makes the air extremely clean and fresh. The local people are very friendly and always help each other with farming. However, the thing I dislike is that there are not many convenience stores or large supermarkets here. We have to travel a long distance to the nearest town just to buy basic things.

Although it is sometimes inconvenient, I still love my village because it is a quiet and beautiful place to live.

*Sample Paragraph 2* (108 từ)

I live in Long Hai, a lively fishing town located just a short drive from Ho Chi Minh City.

There are several reasons why I enjoy living in my neighbourhood. The beach is beautiful, and I can go swimming and watch the sunset every afternoon. The seafood here is always fresh, delicious, and very cheap. However, the major thing I dislike is that it gets very noisy and crowded with tourists during the weekends. The streets become filled with traffic, and there is a lot of litter left on the beach.

Overall, I still love my neighbourhood because the fresh ocean wind makes me feel relaxed and healthy.

### II. PRACTICE

Write a complete paragraph: *Write a paragraph of about 50 words about your neighbourhood, saying what you like and dislike about it.*

## Unit 5 – Natural Wonders of Viet Nam

**Topic:** Write a paragraph of about 50 words about a natural wonder in Viet Nam that you know.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **What?** – What is the name of the natural wonder? (*Tên của kỳ quan thiên nhiên đó là gì?*)
- **Where?** – Where is it located and how can you get there? (*Nó nằm ở đâu và làm thế nào em có thể đến đó?*)
- **What?** – What is special or beautiful about its scenery? (*Cảnh quan ở đó có gì đặc biệt hoặc đẹp đẽ?*)
- **What?** – What activities can you do when you visit this place? (*Em có thể làm những hoạt động gì khi đến thăm nơi này?*)
- **How?** – How do you feel about this natural wonder? (*Em cảm thấy thế nào về kỳ quan thiên nhiên này?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Topic Sentence)**: Giới thiệu tên và vị trí của kỳ quan thiên nhiên.  
   Ví dụ: *I am writing about Ba Be Lake, a famous natural wonder in Bac Kan Province.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Miêu tả cảnh đẹp tự nhiên và các hoạt động du khách có thể làm.  
   Ví dụ: *It is a large freshwater lake surrounded by high limestone mountains and green forests. Visitors can take a boat trip, explore caves, or go hiking in the national park.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Concluding Sentence)**: Bày tỏ tình cảm, suy nghĩ của em về địa điểm đó.  
   Ví dụ: *I love this lake very much because it is peaceful and the air is very clean.*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **Natural Features:** lake, forest, cave, waterfall, valley, bay, beach, island, rock column, landscape, scenery
- **Adjectives:** peaceful, quiet, stunning, freshwater, deep, clean, green, fresh, rocky, natural
- **Activities:** take a boat trip, explore caves, go hiking, watch birds, take photos, ride a raft, swim in the sea
- **Rules & Actions:** follow instructions, protect nature, wear a life jacket, do not litter, do not feed animals
- **Sentence starters:**
  - I am writing about...
  - It is located in...
  - You can travel there by...
  - This place is special because...
  - When visiting this wonder, you must... and mustn't...

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (109 từ)

Ba Be Lake is a stunning natural wonder located in Bac Kan Province, in the north of Viet Nam.

It is a large freshwater lake surrounded by high limestone mountains and green forests. The water here is clean and blue, reflecting the sky like a giant mirror. When visiting this lake, you can take a boat tour to explore the area. You can also walk along the mountain trails to see wild plants and flowers. Remember that you must protect nature and you mustn't litter during your trip.

I love Ba Be Lake because it is peaceful and beautiful. It is a perfect destination for anyone who loves nature.

*Sample Paragraph 2* (108 từ)

Can Gio Mangrove Forest is an amazing natural wonder situated in the south of Ho Chi Minh City.

It is a green wetland area filled with thousands of tall mangrove trees. You can travel there by bus or motorbike. The forest is home to many wild animals, especially friendly monkeys and colourful birds. Visitors can take a canoe trip down the river to see the beautiful landscape. You must wear a life jacket when riding the canoe, and you mustn't feed the wild animals.

I really enjoy visiting Can Gio because the fresh air makes me feel relaxed. It is a wonderful green getaway from the busy city.

### II. PRACTICE

## Unit 6 – Our Tet Holiday

**Topic:** Write an email (about 80-100 words) to a penfriend about what children should and shouldn't do at Tet in Viet Nam.

### I. LEARN

**1. How to Write (Cách viết)**

*Step 1: Brainstorm ideas (Lên ý tưởng)*

- **Who?** – Who should you write this email to? (*Em nên viết lá thư điện tử này cho ai?*)
- **What?** – What is the main topic of the email? (*Chủ đề chính của lá thư điện tử này là gì?*)
- **What?** – What should children do to help their families prepare for Tet? (*Trẻ em nên làm gì để giúp gia đình chuẩn bị đón Tết?*)
- **What?** – What should children not do during the Tet holiday? (*Trẻ em không nên làm gì trong kỳ nghỉ Tết?*)
- **Why?** – Why is it important to follow these customs? (*Tại sao việc tuân theo những phong tục này lại quan trọng?*)

*Step 2: Outline (Lên dàn ý)*

1. **Mở bài (Introduction / Topic Sentence)**: Giới thiệu về dịp Tết và dẫn dắt vào chủ đề những việc trẻ em nên làm hoặc không nên làm.  
   Ví dụ: *Dear Alex, Tet is coming, so I will tell you about what children should and shouldn't do during this holiday in Viet Nam.*
2. **Thân bài (Body / Supporting Sentences)**: Nêu chi tiết những việc nên làm (ví dụ: chúc Tết, giúp đỡ bố mẹ) và không nên làm (ví dụ: cãi vặt, làm vỡ đồ đạc).  
   Ví dụ: *We should help our parents decorate the house and say polite wishes to our elders. However, we shouldn't make noise or ask for lucky money.*
3. **Kết luận (Conclusion / Concluding Sentence)**: Nêu lời chúc hoặc mời bạn chia sẻ về ngày lễ ở nước của họ.  
   Ví dụ: *I hope you will visit Viet Nam during Tet someday. Write to me soon!*

*Step 3: Write a draft (Viết bài)* · *Step 4: Review and edit (Kiểm tra và chỉnh sửa)* — xem quy trình chung ở đầu tài liệu.

**2. Useful Vocabulary and Phrases (Từ vựng và cụm từ hữu ích)**

- **Preparations (Chuẩn bị):** clean the house, decorate the living room, buy flowers, display apricot blossoms
- **Good activities (Hoạt động tốt):** behave well, say polite wishes, visit grandparents, give lucky money
- **Bad activities (Hoạt động không tốt):** break things, argue with siblings, stay up late, eat too many sweets
- **Tet symbols (Biểu tượng ngày Tết):** red envelopes, peach blossoms, traditional food, family gathering
- **Feelings & Wishes (Cảm xúc & Lời chúc):** excited, happy, healthy, full of fun
- **Sentence starters:**
  - Dear [Friend's Name],
  - Tet is coming, so I want to tell you about...
  - At Tet, we should... because...
  - We should also..., too.
  - However, we shouldn't... or...

**3. Sample Paragraph (Bài viết mẫu)**

*Sample Paragraph 1* (115 từ)

Dear David, I hope you are well. Tet, our traditional New Year, is coming to Hanoi, and I am excited to tell you about what children in northern Viet Nam should and shouldn't do during this special holiday.

At Tet, we should help our parents decorate the house with beautiful pink peach blossoms and clean our rooms. We should also behave politely and wish our grandparents good health to receive lucky money. However, we shouldn't eat too many sweet treats or play computer games all day.

We shouldn't stay up too late either, as we need to stay healthy for the holiday. Please write to me soon and tell me about your holidays! Yours, Minh.
